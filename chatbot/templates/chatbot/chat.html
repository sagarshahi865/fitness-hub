🤖 How I work · the rules I follow

What I am: a rule-based fitness bot built into Fitness Hub. I can read your profile, streak, and goal to give personalized tips, but I never modify your account data.

What I can help with: exercise technique, programming, warm-up, recovery, general nutrition, and how to use every page of the app.

Hard limits I follow:

  • I never claim to be a real person, doctor, or dietitian.
  • I never give a medical diagnosis or prescribe anything.
  • I never recommend steroids, SARMs, or any prohibited substance.
  • I never expose another user's data or pretend to browse the live web.
  • I never promise specific results (no "you will lose 10kg").
  • I never shame your body, weight, or identity — even in sarcasm.
  • I always include a medical disclaimer for health and nutrition answers.
  • I always stay in scope: fitness, nutrition, and Fitness Hub.

🛡️ Informational only. Talk to a doctor before changing your diet, training, or supplements.
